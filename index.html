Tips
  • Start from the example: change one section at a time and watch the sheet.
  • Name the problem, not the solution.
  • One cause per row. Keep asking “why” until you reach a process cause.
  • Use numbers with units, and measure the result the same way as the baseline.
  • To print: choose A3 landscape, margins None, and turn on Background graphics.
  • Your work is saved in this browser only. Use Save file to back up or move it to another device.
  • On a phone, switch between the Edit and Preview tabs. Press Esc to close this menu.
